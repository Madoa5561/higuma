# Code of Conduct

We want higuma to be a respectful, practical, and welcoming project.

Participants are expected to:

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Assume good intent while remaining precise about technical concerns.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing private information.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect users and the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, and sustained disruptive behavior are not
accepted. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Reports should be made privately through the repository's security or maintainer
contact channel once the repository is published.
